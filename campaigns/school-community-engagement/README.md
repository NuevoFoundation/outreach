# School and Community Engagement

This campaign combines a personalized outreach email with a shareable one-page flyer for families, educators, schools, and community organizations.

## Review the Kit

Open [preview.html](preview.html) in a browser. Use the **Flyer** and **Email** tabs to review both formats, and use **Print preview** to export the flyer as a PDF.

## Personalize Outreach

Update only the recipient name, organization, and one researched connection near the beginning of the email. Keep the remaining campaign language reusable and approved.

The included Lakeview School District recipient is fictional and exists only as an example.

## Before Publishing

1. Replace the email request link with the approved engagement form URL when available.
2. Host the exported flyer at a stable public URL and update **View the flyer** in the email.
3. Confirm impact metrics and contact details with the campaign owner.
4. Test all links and review the flyer at US Letter size.

Do not commit real recipient details, contact lists, or outreach tracking data.
